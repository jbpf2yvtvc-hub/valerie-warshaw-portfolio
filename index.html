About

Valerie Warshaw

I am a certified interior designer who knows how to guide the whole project team from the kick-off to the punch list.

I like to work with clients and coworkers alike to solve creative and special problems, and I can do this on multiple simultaneous projects without missing a beat.

I have more than 20 years of experience in commercial interiors. I spent 18 of them at Baskervill in Richmond, first as an Interior Designer and then as a Senior Interior Designer. Before that, I worked at KSA Interiors as an Interior Designer and Junior Project Manager. My work covers workplace, hospitality, and education projects, including leading the design of hotels for Marriott and Hilton brands from schematics through construction administration.
